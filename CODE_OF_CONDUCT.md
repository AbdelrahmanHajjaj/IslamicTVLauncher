# Code of conduct · ميثاق السلوك

Afnon is made for people at prayer time, and the people who build it should treat each other with the same
calm and respect the app tries to show. This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, in
Arabic or English.

<p dir="rtl">أفنون مصنوع لأوقات الصلاة، ونريد أن يتعامل من يبنونه مع بعضهم بالهدوء والاحترام نفسيهما.
ينطبق هذا على المشكلات وطلبات الدمج والنقاشات والمراجعات، بالعربية أو الإنجليزية.</p>

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Android, to GitHub or to English.
- Critique the work, not the person. Explain why, and suggest what would be better.
- Where scholars differ (Asr schools, high-latitude rules, Hijri sighting), Afnon offers a choice. Discuss
  rulings with respect and never mock anyone's madhab, practice or level of religiosity.
- Accept that the maintainer has the final say on what fits the project, and that "not a fit" is not a
  judgement of you.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, threats or personal attacks.
- Sectarian, racist, sexist or other demeaning remarks, or takfir of anyone.
- Posting someone's private details (address, location, Wi-Fi names, photos) without their permission.
- Sexual content, or spam and advertising.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after the maintainer has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code privately to the maintainer, [@egyjs](https://github.com/egyjs), through
the contact details on their GitHub profile. Say what happened and link to it. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and warn, temporarily block or
permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, depending on how serious and how repeated it is.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
